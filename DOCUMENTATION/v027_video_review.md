## Track and corner changes

Develop local corner character first: worn inner cuts, several alternative curved paths where visible, uneven dividing ridges and asymmetric outer support. Extend these through approach and exit. Re-evaluate the perceived track width after visible riding boundaries exist; an empty uniform dirt expanse can look wider than its actual usable line.

For jumps, start with the identifiable flag/frontage sector, which corresponds to source R01: the authored flag at (326,171) lies nearest route station 26.20 m inside R01. This identifies the sector, not a measured discrepancy in height or gap. Compare the same approach, crest and landing positions before refining any shoulder or runout. Retain accepted v026 R10/T09/R11 macro geometry as the initial baseline.

The v026 crash around 1:50 and the different field of view/riding lines do not establish a geometry fault. Other turns still need a confident mapping to numbered source turns before physical edits. No global narrowing, steeper-all-berms pass, replacement centerline or blanket jump-height change is recommended from these clips alone.
