## Surface and scenery

v005's green gaps and thin, dispersed tree crowns made the setting feel too
park-like. v006 uses a pale sandy infield, broader earth shoulders, muted brown
racing soil, subtle worked lines and fuller perimeter woods. Large trees have
been removed from the open central infield. The three aerial pond positions,
fence, poles and paddock remain orientation landmarks.

Terrain support for each jump is now calculated independently of the globally
nearest lane. This prevents a mound being abruptly cut off when another lane
becomes closer. The jump-to-ground transition spreads over seven metres beyond
the racing shoulder. Berms follow actual route curvature. Shallow starter lines
are at most about 4.5 cm deep; there are no baked braking-bump sections.
